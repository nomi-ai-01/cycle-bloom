About & privacy

Cycle dates, notes, and preferences stay in this browser on this device. No account, analytics, ad trackers, or cycle-data cloud sync. Someone with access to your unlocked device or browser storage may access this information.

Predictions can be inaccurate. Do not use this app as contraception or medical advice. See a clinician for irregular or painful periods.

Background notifications are not enabled: static sites cannot reliably run a daily timer while closed. A push provider and server scheduler are needed.
